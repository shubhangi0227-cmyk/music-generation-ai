How it's built

MIDI → tokenizer → LSTM (NumPy) → sampler → MIDI + WAV

  • 28 procedurally generated MIDI files — classical and jazz
  • Single-layer LSTM with embedding, roughly 49K parameters
  • Trained with Adam and backpropagation through time, no ML framework
  • Audio rendered with additive sine-wave synthesis and ADSR envelopes
